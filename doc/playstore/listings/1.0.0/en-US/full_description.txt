Arthur (ART'hur) turns your phone into a Control Plane for ambient art on Android Auto and Android TV.

Two modes in one app: a museum Gallery of curated works and photos, and Generative art with living genart and fractals. Switch anytime. Prepare a rotation on your phone, then enjoy it on car and living-room canvases.

Free tier includes a curated pack, Fractal Presets, and Auto/TV canvases with a limited pool. Premium unlocks Personal Photos, full Remote Sources, unlimited Genart, and Custom Fractal authoring.
