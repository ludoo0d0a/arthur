# Famous-art Remote Source: Rijksmuseum first, Met in v1.1

v1 needs one solid museum connector behind the Source interface, not several half-wired catalogs. **Rijksmuseum** is the v1 Remote Source (existing KMP samples as learning reference, not a product fork). **The Met** follows in **v1.1**. Wikimedia/Unsplash/Pexels are not the v1 famous-art connector.

Separately in **v1.1**, add one **Stock Photo Source** (**Pexels** or **Unsplash**) for ambient photography — not as a museum/famous-art substitute. Choose a single provider at implementation time based on ToS, attribution, and rate limits.
