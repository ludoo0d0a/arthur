Arthur — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-17

Arthur (“ART'hur”) is an ambient art application for phone, Android Auto, and Android TV operated by GeoKing.

Data we process

  • **Artwork metadata** from museum APIs (e.g. Rijksmuseum) and bundled packs.
  • **Personal photos** you explicitly select (Premium): stored on-device and, if you pair a TV, transferred over your local network to that TV. We do not upload personal photos to our servers.
  • **Purchase status** via RevenueCat / Google Play to unlock Premium.
  • **Diagnostics** (Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics): crash logs and coarse usage events. No advertising ID sale.

    • Android Auto / TV

    Prepared rotations are configured on the phone. Auto uses the Media experience; TV may receive a rotation via local pairing.
