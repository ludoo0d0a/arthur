Arthur — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-04

Arthur (“ART'hur”) is an ambient art application for phone, Android Auto, and Android TV operated by GeoKing.

Data we process

  • Artwork metadata from museum APIs (e.g. Rijksmuseum, The Met), stock photo APIs (Pexels, Unsplash), and bundled packs.
  • Personal photos you explicitly select (Premium): stored on-device and, if you pair a TV, transferred over your local network to that TV. We do not upload personal photos to our servers.
  • Purchase status via RevenueCat / Google Play to unlock Premium.
  • Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics): crash logs and coarse usage events. No sale of advertising ID for ads.

Android Auto / TV

Prepared rotations are configured on the phone. Auto uses the Media experience; TV may receive a rotation via local pairing (LAN / QR).
