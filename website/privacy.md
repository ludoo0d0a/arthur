# Arthur — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-11-22

Arthur (“ART'hur”) is an ambient art application for phone, Android Auto, and Android TV operated by GeoKing.

## Data we process

- **Artwork metadata** from museum APIs (e.g. Rijksmuseum, The Met, Art Institute of Chicago, Cleveland Museum of Art), stock-photo APIs (Pexels, Unsplash when configured), and bundled packs.
- **Personal photos** you explicitly select (when that pack is available): stored on-device and, if you pair a TV, transferred over your local network to that TV. We do not upload personal photos to our servers.
- **Purchase status** via RevenueCat / Google Play to unlock Premium and content packs.
- **Advertising** (Play Store builds, free tier on phone): Google AdMob may show banner ads and use the advertising ID for ad serving. Arthur Premium removes ads. See [Google’s advertising privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- **Diagnostics** (Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics): crash logs and coarse usage events.

## Android Auto / TV

Prepared rotations are configured on the phone. Auto uses the Media experience; TV may receive a rotation via local pairing.
